## Identités créées pour le portfolio

Maritime, Maison Web, HouseConnect, ASTRONET, Timekeeper, Agro, EduPro, Dashboard, ProMessenger, Mova et Terra Immo : onze symboles créés avec l’outil intégré Image Gen. Ils servent d’identités de présentation aux projets qui n’avaient pas de logo repéré. Ils ne sont pas présentés comme des logos officiels fournis par un client.

Planche originale : `docs/assets/generated-brand-marks.png`. Les onze symboles ont été extraits de leurs cellules et enregistrés individuellement en PNG transparent. Le texte des noms reste du texte HTML pour conserver sa lisibilité.

## Consigne de génération

Create a production-ready sheet of ELEVEN distinct, mature, minimal vector-like brand symbols for real software portfolio projects. Transparent PNG with true alpha, no background. Exact uniform grid: 3 columns by 4 rows, twelve equal cells; final bottom-right cell intentionally empty and transparent. Every symbol centered in its cell, same optical weight, occupying only the central 55% of the cell with generous clear transparent margins, no overlap between cells. No text, no letters written as labels, no frames, no tiles, no shadows, no glow, no gradients, no 3D. Crisp clean flat geometric shapes, suitable at a small size on white. Each should read as a designed logomark rather than an emoji or a stock icon. Navy #0c172b primary with one restrained project-specific accent. Row 1 col 1: maritime surveillance, abstract interlocking wave and compass geometry, navy and teal. Row 1 col 2: Maison Web stock-management, architectural M formed from two offset inventory blocks, navy and orange. Row 1 col 3: HouseConnect home automation, continuous geometric house shape integrated with a connection node, navy and teal. Row 2 col 1: ASTRONET astronomical analysis, sharp A-like orbital symbol with one small orbiting dot, navy and cobalt. Row 2 col 2: Timekeeper attendance software, a precise circular time ring integrated with a check, navy and burnt orange. Row 2 col 3: Agro livestock management, abstract cattle horns flowing into one leaf, forest green and sage. Row 3 col 1: EduPro school API, two book pages meeting in an upward geometric opening, navy and cyan. Row 3 col 2: Dashboard task-management API, asymmetrical three-part modular grid, navy and orange. Row 3 col 3: ProMessenger direct messaging, two interlocking angular speech ribbons, navy and blue. Row 4 col 1: Mova secured payment, two balanced interlocking folded paths suggesting an M and an exchange, navy and muted violet. Row 4 col 2: Terra Immo land property, abstract T built from three topographic parcel lines, dark olive and terracotta. Row 4 col 3: completely blank transparent. Output a tall 3:4 composition, clean precise alignment, high-quality edges.
